# Opt-Out and Switch Timing Rules

**Last Updated:** 2026-10-28  
**Researched by:** the owner  
**Sources:** SDG&E tariffs, SDG&E meter-read calendars, CEA board packets, CPUC notices (see bottom for links)

This document tracks the specific timing requirements, deadlines, and cost implications for switching providers, plans, and programs in San Diego County.

---

## 1. Community Choice Aggregation (CCA) Opt-Out to SDG&E

### Clean Energy Alliance (CEA)

**Advance Notice Period:**
- **VERIFIED:** 6 months' advance notice required to return to SDG&E bundled service without transitional rate
- Counted from date SDG&E receives the notice (not date sent to CEA)
- **Source:** SDG&E Electric Rule 27 §L.1, L.2.b, L.3.b (Sheet 20, effective 7/11/2015); SDG&E opt-out page (accessed 2026-10-28)

**Switch Date:**
- **VERIFIED:** Return happens at the first scheduled meter read after the 6-month period ends
- Must compute notice-received-by date = (target meter read date - 6 months)
- Use SDG&E's published meter-read schedule by billing cycle
- **Example (Cycle 19, fixture household):**
  - Today: Oct 28, 2026 (true-up date)
  - Target switch: May 28, 2027 (before June 1 summer pricing)
  - Notice must reach SDG&E by: **Nov 27, 2026**
  - Recommend submitting via CEA by: **~Nov 13, 2026** (2-week buffer for CEA forwarding time)
  - If SDG&E receives after Nov 27: switch pushed to Jun 29, 2027 → one expensive summer cycle on CEA (~$80-105 extra)
- **Source:** SDG&E Rule 27 §L.3.b(1); derived from official SDG&E 2026/2027 meter-read calendars

**Cancellation Window:**
- **VERIFIED:** 3 business days after SDG&E receives notice to cancel, then binding
- **Source:** SDG&E Rule 27 §L.3.b

**Confirmation:**
- **VERIFIED:** SDG&E must confirm within 10 business days of receiving notice
- **Source:** SDG&E Rule 27 §L.3.b

**Minimum Stay Requirements:**
- **VERIFIED:** 12 months minimum on SDG&E bundled service after return
- Time on Transitional Bundled Service (TBS) counts toward the 12 months
- No early departure allowed
- **Source:** SDG&E Rule 27 §L.3.a

**Transitional Bundled Service (TBS) Rate:**
- **VERIFIED:** Returning without 6 months' notice puts customer on TBS until 6 months after SDG&E receives request
- TBS is hourly CAISO market price plus adders
- PCIA and franchise fees still apply on TBS (per CEA)
- **Cost for solar homes on TBS: UNVERIFIED** - winter cost and export credit structure not documented
- **Source:** SDG&E Rule 27 §L.3.c, §M.11; Schedule EECC-TBS; CEA Myths #4

**Opt-Out Fees:**
- **VERIFIED:** CEA charges no exit fee
- **VERIFIED:** SDG&E one-time re-entry fee: ~$1 per account
  - CEA states $0.56 (as of May 2022)
  - SDG&E Schedule CCA lists $1.12 per account
  - Sources disagree on exact amount
- Fee-free only within 60 days of initial CEA enrollment (Oceanside enrolled Apr 2024, window has passed)
- **Source:** CEA opt-out page; CEA notice letter; Schedule CCA Sheet 2 #14

**CEA Processing Time:**
- **VERIFIED (for opt-outs):** Request must arrive ≥5 business days before meter-read date
- Changes happen only on meter-read dates
- **CEA forwarding time to SDG&E: UNVERIFIED** - recommend 2-week buffer
- **Source:** CEA Oceanside/Vista notice letter

**Best Timing:**
- Submit notice to reach SDG&E before the meter read that is 6 months before your target return date
- For before-summer return: calculate backward from May meter read
- **Do NOT act immediately post-true-up** - this forces transitional rate or misses notice deadline

### San Diego Community Power (SDCP)

**Status:** Likely similar structure to CEA (same SDG&E Rule 27 applies), but SDCP-specific forwarding times and fees need independent verification
- Advance notice: Same SDG&E Rule 27 (6 months)
- Minimum stay: Same SDG&E Rule 27 (12 months)
- Processing time: UNVERIFIED

## 2. CCA Tier Changes

### CEA Clean Impact Plus → Clean Impact (Opt-Down)

**Effective Date:**
- **VERIFIED (for opt-outs, likely applies to opt-downs):** Changes happen only on meter-read dates
- Request must arrive ≥5 business days before the meter read
- **Example (Cycle 19, Oct 28, 2026):** Today's read is too late; next read is Nov 27. Last day to request: **Thu Nov 19, 2026** (Nov 26 is Thanksgiving)
- **Source:** CEA Oceanside/Vista notice letter (stated for opt-outs); CEA opt-down page

**Processing:**
- **VERIFIED:** Online form at thecleanenergyalliance.org/opt-down/ or call 833-232-3110
- **VERIFIED:** Can switch back to Plus any time (no restrictions)
- **Source:** CEA opt-down page

**Relief Credit Expiration:**
- **VERIFIED:** Clean Impact relief credit ends **December 31, 2026**
- **VERIFIED:** Residential credit: $0.03871/kWh (as stated Feb 1, 2026; CEA board reports adjustments since Feb, so current TOU-DR1 value may differ slightly)
- **Impact:** For Cycle 19 fixture household requesting by Nov 19 → credit applies only to Nov 27–Dec 29 billing period → worth approximately **$3-6** (one winter cycle, not $10-30/month)
- Credit extension into 2027 is **UNVERIFIED** (CEA says it "will reassess rates before the end of the year")
- **Source:** CEA rate relief release (2/19/2026); CEA rate schedule effective 2/1/2026; CEA 10/29/2026 board packet

**Recommended Action:**
- Request by Nov 19, 2026 if switching to Clean Impact
- Current value is minimal (one winter billing cycle only)
- Free and reversible, so no downside to opting down
- Do not claim "$10-30/month starting Jan 2027" - credit expires Dec 31

**Timing:**
- Same meter-read and 5-business-day lead time rules likely apply

## 3. NEM/Solar and CCA Switch Interactions

### Interim True-Up Trigger

**VERIFIED (SDG&E and CEA):**
- **Switching providers triggers an interim true-up** of your NEM account
- The 12-month NEM cycle is **terminated early** at the switch date
- You are billed for any outstanding balance or credited for excess generation
- A **new 12-month cycle begins** with the new provider, with new anniversary date
- **Both SDG&E and CEA true up separately** on the switch
- **Source:** SDG&E Schedule NEM-ST (NEM 2.0) Special Condition 3, 3(f); SDG&E opt-out page; CEA NEM Terms & Conditions §H

**Credit Forfeiture:**
- **VERIFIED:** Credits left in the partial billing period are forfeited (not carried to new provider)
- SDG&E Net Surplus Compensation (NSC) paid only for net-surplus kWh at end of true-up period
- CEA credits are zeroed; NSC of $0.06/kWh paid by check only if net surplus kWh and ≥$100
- **Example (Cycle 19 household):** Switching May 28, 2027 forfeits Oct 29, 2026–May 28, 2027 spring solar credits. Estimated lost credits: ~$50-90 (based on prior year Apr-May credits of $48-88)
- This cost should be subtracted from "leave CEA" savings estimates
- **Source:** SDG&E NEM-ST SC 3, 3(h); CEA NEM T&C E.3; CEA rate schedule

**REMOVED RULE (Incorrect):**
- ~~"Switch within 60 days after true-up"~~ - This rule is unsourced and incorrect
- Acting immediately post-true-up would put customer on transitional rate or miss notice deadline window
- Correct approach: Calculate notice deadline based on target meter-read date (6 months prior)

### SDG&E Rate Plan Changes with NEM

**VERIFIED:**
- Changing SDG&E rate plans (e.g., TOU-DR1 → TOU-DR2) **does NOT trigger interim true-up** as long as you stay on SDG&E
- Rate plan changes take effect after the next regular meter read following the request
- **Limitation:** Can only change rate plans **once every 12 months**
- **Source:** SDG&E Rule 12 §D; SDG&E pricing-plan FAQ

**Available Plans for NEM 2.0 + CCA:**
- **VERIFIED:** TOU-DR1, TOU-DR2, DR-SES, EV-TOU-5 (EV), TOU-ELEC (EV/storage/heat pump)
- **NOT available to CCA/TBS customers:** TOU-DR-P, EV-TOU-5-P
- NEM-ST customers must stay on TOU plans
- **Source:** SDG&E pricing-plan page; NEM-ST Rates

**Important Timing Consideration:**
- **Do NOT change rate plans before a CCA switch**
- Best plan on CEA (e.g., DR-SES has lower CEA generation rates in summer than TOU-DR1) can differ from best plan on SDG&E bundled service
- Changing now locks you in for 12 months
- Save the one allowed change per year for just before returning to SDG&E
- **Source:** internal analysis; SDG&E Rule 12 §D

## 5. CARE/FERA Enrollment and Renewals

### Initial Enrollment

**Timing:**
- **VERIFIED:** No enrollment deadline
- Takes 2-3 billing cycles to process initial application
- **Retroactive:** Discounts applied retroactively to application date once approved
- **Source:** sdge.com/CARE

**Recommended Action:**
- **Apply immediately** if eligible (no cost to apply, only benefits)
- Each month delayed costs ~$30-50 in lost discounts
- **Annual recertification:** Required every 1-2 years depending on income documentation method

### Income Verification

**Self-Attestation:**
- **VERIFIED:** Customers can self-attest income under oath
- **Expiration:** 12-month recertification required
- **Penalty:** Providing false information is perjury
- **Source:** CA PUC Decision D.20-07-032; SDG&E CARE/FERA enrollment page

## 6. True-Up Bill Amount (For Solar/NEM Customers)

**Monthly Payment Detection:**
- **IMPORTANT:** If customer pays monthly (not banking credits), the true-up bill is only the unbilled final period charges, NOT the cumulative balance shown on the bill
- **Example (Cycle 19 fixture):** Bill shows NEM YTD balance $445.71, but customer paid $354.22 on 8/21/26. True-up bill will be approximately the unpaid Sep 28–Oct 28 charges (~$90), not the full $445.71
- The printed "NEM Year-to-Date Balance" is cumulative; paid amounts reduce what's actually owed
- **Source:** a sample customer bill; SDG&E billing practices

**Detection Method:**
- Check bill history for payments
- If payments roughly equal monthly charges → customer pays monthly → true-up is small
- If no payments until true-up → customer banks credits → true-up is full balance
