## 2. CCA Tier Changes

### CEA Clean Impact Plus → Clean Impact (Opt-Down)

**Effective Date:**
- **Verified (CEA website, 2026-10-28):** Tier changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle after request
- **Processing time:** 1-2 billing cycles typical for enrollment changes

**Relief Credit Expiration:**
- **Verified (CEA 2026 announcement):** Clean Impact relief credit ends **December 31, 2026**
- **Impact:** Clean Impact rate increases ~$0.01-0.02/kWh starting January 2027
- **Action window:** Customers on Clean Impact Plus should evaluate switching down before Dec 31, 2026 to capture remaining relief credit months

**Recommended Action Date:**
- **For households where Clean Impact Plus is more expensive:** Request opt-down by **November 1, 2026** to ensure December billing cycle captures last month of relief credit
- **Cost of waiting:** Miss relief credit months (~$10-30/month depending on usage)

### CEA Clean Impact → Clean Impact Plus (Opt-Up)

**Timing:**
- Same 1-2 billing cycle processing
- No known expiring credits, but verify current incentives

## 3. NEM/Solar and CCA Switch Interactions

### Interim True-Up Trigger

**Verified (SDG&E NEM 2.0 tariff, Section 5.3):**
- **Switching CCAs triggers an interim true-up** of your NEM account
- The 12-month NEM cycle is **terminated early**, and you are billed for any outstanding balance or credited for excess generation
- A **new 12-month cycle begins** with the new CCA

**Cost Implications:**
- **Worst case:** Switching in late winter/spring (Feb-May) when you have low NEM balance means you pay off small balance and restart cycle before high summer generation → lose benefit of summer surplus against winter usage
- **Best case:** Switch right after true-up date (within 1-2 months) to minimize disruption

**Recommended Timing for Solar Customers:**
- **Optimal window:** Within **60 days after** your annual true-up date
- **Avoid:** Switching 6-8 months before your true-up date (mid-cycle disruption)
- **Example:** True-up date Oct 28 → safe window to switch CCA is Nov 1 - Dec 31

**Source:** SDG&E Electric Rule 27 (Net Energy Metering), Section 5.3 "Customer-Initiated Service Changes"

### SDG&E Rate Plan Changes with NEM

**Verified (SDG&E NEM rules):**
- Changing SDG&E rate plans (e.g., TOU-DR1 → TOU-DR2) **does NOT trigger interim true-up** as long as you stay on SDG&E
- Rate plan changes take effect at the start of next billing cycle
- **Limitation:** Can only change rate plans **once every 12 months**

## 4. SDG&E Rate Plan Changes (Non-NEM)

### TOU-DR1 ↔ Other TOU Plans

**Change Frequency:**
- **Verified (SDG&E tariffs):** Residential customers can change rate plans **once per 12-month period**
- **Effective date:** Beginning of next billing cycle after request received

**Bill Protection:**
- **Verified (SDG&E 2026 rate plan info):** New TOU customers get **12-month bill protection** comparing new TOU rate to prior rate schedule
- **Eligibility:** First-time switchers to TOU only; does not apply to subsequent changes

**Processing:**
- Online requests via sdge.com take 1-2 billing cycles to process
- Phone requests may be faster (needs verification)

**Best Timing:**
- **Summer → Winter shift (Sept-Oct):** Switch to less expensive off-peak heavy plan before winter when usage shifts indoors
- **Winter → Summer shift (March-April):** Switch to plans with better peak/super off-peak splits if you can shift usage or have solar

## 5. CARE/FERA Enrollment and Renewals

### Initial Enrollment

**Timing:**
- **Verified (SDG&E CARE/FERA page):** Takes 2-3 billing cycles to process initial application
- **Retroactive:** Discounts applied retroactively to application date once approved

**Recommended Action:**
- **Apply immediately** if eligible (no cost to apply, only benefits)
- **Annual recertification:** Required every 1-2 years depending on income documentation method

### Income Verification

**Self-Attestation:**
- **Verified (CA PUC CARE rules):** Customers can self-attest income under oath
- **Expiration:** 12-month recertification required
- **Penalty:** Providing false information is perjury

**Source:** CA PUC Decision D.20-07-032, SDG&E CARE/FERA enrollment page

## Summary of Action Windows

| Action | Optimal Timing | Deadline/Avoid | Cost of Missing |
|--------|---------------|----------------|-----------------|
| CEA Clean Impact Plus → Impact | By Nov 1, 2026 | Dec 31, 2026 relief credit ends | ~$10-30/month starting Jan 2027 |
| CCA switch (solar customers) | Within 60 days after true-up | Avoid 6-8 months before true-up | Interim true-up disruption, lost summer credits |
| CCA switch (non-solar) | Before summer (by May) | Avoid June-Sept enrollment | Higher summer rates while processing |
| SDG&E rate plan change | March-April or Sept-Oct | Can only change 1x/12mo | Locked into suboptimal plan for 12 months |
| CARE/FERA enrollment | Immediately if eligible | No deadline, but lose months of discounts | ~$30-50/month for each month delayed |
